### Upgrade notes
- Deprecation: `GET /v1/graph` keeps returning the full response by default in 0.115.x, and 0.116.0 makes the new `view=summary` projection the default. A client that reads node attributes, `evidence_provenance` or edge ids from `/v1/graph` should pass `view=full` explicitly before upgrading. `view=summary` (alias `view=lean`, labelled `view: "summary"`) carries each node's `id`, `entity_type`, `label`, `severity`, `risk_score`, `status` and only the attributes that name and badge it; each edge's `source`, `target`, `relationship`, `direction`, `weight`, `traversable` and `confidence` (an edge's `id` is always `{relationship}:{source}:{target}`); and a summary of each embedded attack path. A node's full attributes and `evidence_provenance` come from `GET /v1/graph/node/{node_id}`, complete paths from `GET /v1/graph/attack-paths`; `include=attributes` or `include=paths` restores one of the two within the summary. The dashboard's graph page requests `view=canvas` and is unchanged.

### Performance
- `GET /v1/graph?view=summary` is 5 to 7 times smaller than the full response: on the `scan --demo` estate 116 KB instead of 867 KB for 124 nodes and 255 edges (934 instead of 6,994 bytes per node), and on the showcase estate 775 KB instead of 4.35 MB for 1,292 nodes and 1,775 edges (599 instead of 3,367 bytes per node).
