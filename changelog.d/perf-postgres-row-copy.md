### Performance
- On PostgreSQL, a current estate with one source snapshot is copied into the current-graph projection as rows, streamed from server-side cursors in one consistent snapshot, instead of being loaded into graph objects and written back. The copy applies under the same conditions as the SQLite copy (the source is at the revision the build resolved, its agent context and path index are current, and every node carries its recorded identity) and otherwise falls back to the rebuild. That rebuild held the API's interpreter lock through the first graph reads after a large push.
