### Added
- The investigation graph's full map opens as a grouped map, and estates of 200 nodes or more land on it. Nodes collapse into groups by tier and type (scope, agents, MCP servers and tools, packages by ecosystem, findings by severity, identities and credentials, cloud and data resources by type), laid out as columns in that order and wrapped into bands so the whole overview fits the canvas. One bundled edge per group pair carries the exact count per relationship ("Depends On ×38"). The overview draws bundles between neighbouring columns and those carrying a hop of the 10 highest-risk attack paths; the rest appear when a group is pointed at or with "Show all links", and the header counts them. Clicking a group expands it in place into a frame with its top members by risk and a "+K more" pager; finding a node opens its group and focuses it; "Show every edge" returns the ungrouped map.
- `GET /v1/graph/cluster-map` serves the grouped map: bounded, tenant-scoped, with drawn, hidden, inside-group and filtered edge counts that always add up to the snapshot's total.

### Performance
- The grouped map is answered from a per-snapshot aggregation built once per generation from a lean, index-backed read on SQLite and Postgres and warmed when a snapshot commits. At the 5,000-agent qualification estate (170k nodes, 229k edges) a frame takes under 3 ms once warm; the one-time build is about 2 s on SQLite and about 8 s on Postgres.
